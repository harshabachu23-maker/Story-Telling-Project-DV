Chapter 05 • From insight to action

What can the college do next?

Turn the observations into practical support and check whether it helps.

  • Support students who are struggling: arrange mentoring and doubt-clearing sessions.
  • Improve subject learning: offer extra practice and revision in lower-scoring subjects.
  • Understand attendance: speak with students about repeated absences and possible barriers.
  • Build study routines: encourage a realistic, regular timetable rather than last-minute study.
  • Measure progress: compare results again after support is provided.
